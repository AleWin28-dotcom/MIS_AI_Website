Cybersecurity and Information Protection

Cybersecurity is the common practice of protecting computers, their networks, and their systems, and data from unauthorized access or Cyber attacks. Businesses rely heavily on cybersecurity iniatives to protect sensitive information, maintain a sense of customer trust, and keep their operations running smoothly and safely. As organizations become more dependent on technology by the day, strong cybersecurity practices are increasingly important.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

  1. Phishing
  2. Password/credential theft
  3. Data breaches

Real-World Cybersecurity Example

A huge example of a cybersecurity attack is the 2017 WannaCry ransomware attack. WannaCry impacted organizations all around the world by locking computer files, and also demanding payment to restore access. This attack displayed just how important it is for organizations to keep their systems updated, sanctioned, and maintain strong cybersecurity practices.

How Businesses Can Stay Secure

  • Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • Train employees to recognize phishing attempts
  • Limit access to sensitive information
