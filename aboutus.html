About This Website

This website was created to explore the important metrics of Management Information Systems (MIS), including artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. The main purpose of this website is to explore and explain how these technologies are utilized in modern businesses all while also considering the key responsibilities and ethical concerns that can come along with them.

Why This Topic Matters

Technology is ever growing and ever flowing. It continues to change the way businesses operate and just how people interact with organizations. Understanding important topics such as artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity can help future professionals alike make better decisions about how technology should be used ethically, efficiently, and responsibly. Learning about these topics also displays the importance of balancing innovation along with ethical responsibility.
